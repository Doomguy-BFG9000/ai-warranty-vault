AquaWash Service Terms (EU) — edition 2026-01
Applies to AquaWash 8000 series appliances sold in the European Union.

1. Scope
These terms describe how AquaWash authorised service centres handle repairs. They
supplement, and do not replace, the warranty card supplied with the appliance.

2. Response times
A service visit is offered within 5 working days of a reported fault. In the
Warsaw, Krakow and Gdansk metropolitan areas the target is 2 working days.

3. Parts coverage
Replacement parts installed during a warranty repair are covered for the
remainder of the original warranty period, or 90 days, whichever is longer.
The mains power cable and the inlet hose are treated as accessories and are
covered for 12 months from the date of purchase.

4. Transport
Where an appliance must be moved to a service centre, transport is arranged and
paid for by the service centre during the first 24 months.

5. What the customer must provide
Access to the appliance, a description of the fault, the appliance serial number
from the rating plate, and proof of purchase. A repair cannot be booked without
proof of purchase.

6. Out-of-warranty repairs
Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted before work begins. The diagnostic fee is
waived if the quotation is accepted.
