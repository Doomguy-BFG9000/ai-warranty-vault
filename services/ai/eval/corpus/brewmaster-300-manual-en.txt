BrewMaster 300 — espresso machine user guide
Model BM-300. Document revision 2.

Getting started
Fill the water tank to the MAX line with fresh cold water. Do not use sparkling
or distilled water. Insert the water filter cartridge and run two cycles of
water through the brew head before the first coffee.

Descaling
Descale the machine every 300 cups, or every two months in areas with hard
water. Use only the BrewMaster descaling solution. Add one sachet to a full
tank, start the descaling programme from the settings menu and let it run to
completion. Do not interrupt the programme: an interrupted cycle leaves acid in
the circuit and the machine will show E05 until a full cycle completes.

Replacing the water filter
Replace the filter cartridge every two months. Remove the tank, pull the old
cartridge straight up, soak the new one in water for five minutes and press it
into the holder until it clicks.

Error codes
E02 — water tank not detected. Reseat the tank.
E05 — descaling required, or a descaling cycle was interrupted. Run a complete
descaling programme.
E11 — grinder blocked. Switch off, remove the bean hopper and clear the grinder
with the supplied brush. Never use water inside the grinder.
E40 — thermoblock fault. The machine must be serviced.

Cleaning
Empty and rinse the drip tray daily. Clean the brew group weekly with the
supplied tablet. The milk circuit runs an automatic rinse after each use; run
the deep clean programme weekly.
