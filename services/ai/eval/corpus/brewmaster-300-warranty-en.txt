BrewMaster 300 — warranty statement
BrewMaster Appliances Ltd. Reference BW-2026-04.

Cover period
This appliance carries a 12-month warranty from the date of purchase for
household use. For commercial use the warranty period is 6 months.

What is covered
Manufacturing defects and material defects in the pump, the thermoblock and the
electronic control board.

What is not covered
- Damage caused by limescale where the descaling schedule in the user guide was
  not followed.
- Damage caused by using a descaling product other than the one specified.
- Blockages caused by oily or flavoured coffee beans.
- Cosmetic wear, and consumables such as filters, seals and gaskets.

Making a claim
Contact the retailer where the appliance was bought, with the proof of purchase
and the serial number from the base of the machine. Faults reported in the first
30 days are handled as a replacement rather than a repair.
